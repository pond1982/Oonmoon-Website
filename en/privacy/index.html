Privacy notice — owner-review draft

This draft describes the audited Oonmoon 0.7.8 app and the planned static website. It is not yet an approved final policy. The legal operator, effective date, applicable markets and privacy rights, and final English and Thai wording still need the owner's confirmation before release. Privacy questions can be sent to pondd.me@gmail.com. Owner access to Apple-provided app analytics, sales/download reports and diagnostics also remains unconfirmed; these platform reports are separate from embedded app analytics.

Preferences saved on your device

Oonmoon stores character appearance and selection, reading language, sound choices, bookmarks and story-completion preferences locally. Existing character names from earlier setup may also remain in local preferences. The current app implementation does not send these preferences to a developer-operated server. Device backups are a separate matter.

Accounts, tracking and permissions

The audited Release app has no Oonmoon sign-in account, advertising, analytics, tracking or photo-upload feature. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking or developer-collected data. These technical findings do not replace the owner's final policy approval.

Recorded narration

English and Thai narration recordings are bundled with the app and played locally, including offline playback for stories you can access. Reading does not send text, names or audio to a narration provider or generate a new voice recording. Purchases and restoring access may still need a connection to Apple.

Purchases handled by Apple

The one-time full-library purchase uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles the Apple Account and payment flow; Oonmoon uses verified purchase information to decide which stories can be opened. Product details, purchases, restores and ownership updates can communicate with Apple. Restore Purchases may ask for Apple Account authentication. Apple's own privacy terms apply to its services.

Deleting local choices

In the grown-ups area, Delete character & local choices removes the app's saved character preferences, language and sound choices, and bookmarks on that device. It does not erase Apple purchase records, device backups or support emails. Backup behavior depends on your device and Apple settings; no promise is made here about deleting information from backups.

When you email support

If you choose to email pondd.me@gmail.com, your email address, message and any attachments you send are handled through email services, separately from the app. Please send only the details needed to explain the issue. The support mailbox's retention practices and the owner's handling of privacy requests still need confirmation.

Website language preference

This static website uses browser localStorage to remember your explicit English or Thai language choice. You can remove that preference in your browser's site-storage settings. The website has no login, backend, analytics or advertising scripts, and its own code sets no cookies. Browser-language detection is used only to suggest a language on the first visit.

Website hosting is separate

The website is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub's processing is separate from the Oonmoon app and is governed by GitHub's own policies. Hosting-log details, retention and the owner's access to hosting data have not yet been confirmed. The app's local-storage practices should not be read as a promise that website hosting processes no visitor information.
